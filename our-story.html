Our Story

#ElsLockedInEl

Who would have thought that a simple gift exchange at Campus Christian Family (CCF), KNUST, would turn out to be God's way of exchanging two hearts?

It all started when, by some interesting twist of fate (or should we say divine arrangement? 🤭), we were paired for a church gift exchange. She saw a calm, composed, mature gentleman. He saw a God-fearing, virtuous, well-mannered lady. Love at first sight? Not exactly! But somewhere between the conversations the friendship intensified.

What followed was a masterclass in patience! Long "I-was-just-passing by" treks to Baby Brunei Hostel, long distance trips, surprise food deliveries, and countless check-ins. Charley, love is not for the weak!

Someone asked: How did you propose? Well with many signs and wonders lol. First, a family dinner with only one guest and a special seat, then a date with an intentional silence on the much anticipated question to study the atmosphere, and then finally, the question that changed everything although it wasn't answered then.

But beyond the laughter, our greatest blessing has been finding friendship, faith, and unwavering support in each other. Through life's uncertainties, prayers, encouragement, God has continually drawn us closer.

She's his Abigail, his voice of wisdom and encouragement. He's her biggest cheerleader, always pushing her to reach greater heights. Together, we've found a love that's beautifully imperfect, wonderfully balanced, and super blissful!

To our families, amigos, friends, and everyone who has played a part in this journey, thank you!

Now, with grateful hearts, we're ready for our greatest adventure yet: doing life together, God's way.
